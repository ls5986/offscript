Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026. Covers Off Script for iOS and the browser.

Off Script is an improv and conversation-game app. Ordinary gameplay works without an Off Script account or a developer-operated backend. The app includes its games and prompts, and does not use runtime AI, advertising, tracking, or an app-managed analytics service.

What the app stores on your device

Depending on what you use or enter, local app data includes:

  • Player names, party groups, teams, and local player identifiers.
  • Game sessions, roles, results, favorites, and prompt history.
  • Saved prompts, locked ingredients, presets, and Studio settings.
  • Content preferences, accessibility choices, and sound settings.
  • Local ratings and optional feedback notes.

The iOS app stores this data in SQLite inside its app container. The web version uses browser storage, including IndexedDB. Off Script does not automatically upload this gameplay data or player names to the developer, and does not provide app-managed cloud sync.

The browser downloads the app, fonts and bundled game content from the website. A service worker may cache those files for offline access. Gameplay saves are separate from that file cache. Moving to another domain, browser or device does not transfer saved data.

Only enter names and notes that everyone involved is comfortable keeping on the device. Anyone able to access the app on that device may be able to see saved information. A covered private-role card is a gameplay aid, not an account or identity-verification system.

Feedback you choose to share

Ratings and feedback notes stay local unless you choose to share or copy them. A prepared in-app feedback report contains the app version, game identifier, issue or rating, applicable content-pack and ingredient identifiers, and any optional note you wrote. It does not automatically include player names or private-role text.

On iOS, you choose the destination in the system share sheet. On the web, feedback may be copied to your clipboard for you to send. Preparing a report is not automatic submission to the developer. Remove personal information from notes and screenshots before sharing. The service and recipient you choose handle the copy you send.

TestFlight beta testing is different

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects beta usage information and crash logs and shares information with the app developer. This can include the app and OS versions, device information, installations, sessions, crashes, and invitation details. Depending on how you were invited, your name and email address may also be visible to the developer.

TestFlight diagnostics are not the same as Off Script's local feedback ratings. Apple states that testers cannot opt out of TestFlight's automatic diagnostic collection. Screenshots and comments you submit through TestFlight are also shared with the developer.

Beta diagnostics and reports are used to investigate problems and improve Off Script, not for advertising or sale. Apple controls retention in TestFlight. Copies retained outside TestFlight should be kept only while needed for those purposes or a legal obligation. Clearing local app data does not delete information already shared through TestFlight.

See Apple's TestFlight & Privacy for the information Apple collects, invitation differences, retention, and data-request instructions.

Device permissions and backups

Off Script does not record audio or video, and does not request camera or microphone access. Spoken cues use device speech features; sounds, haptics, and speech are controlled by app and device settings.

Device or operating-system backups may include local app data. Off Script does not manage those backups. Backup, restore, and platform-level diagnostic settings are controlled by you and the relevant platform provider.

Keeping and removing local data

Local data remains until you clear it, the app or browser removes it, or a platform storage event affects it. In the app, open Settings > Clear all local data and confirm to reset saved user data. Bundled games and prompts remain available. There is no Off Script account to delete.

Removing the app or clearing browser site data can remove local progress. Clearing data in the app does not erase older device backups or copies you sent elsewhere, and is not a promise of forensic deletion. A restored device backup may restore app data.

Website hosting and third-party sites

The website and browser app are prepared for Vercel hosting. When you visit, the hosting provider processes ordinary request information, which can include your IP address, requested URL, browser information and request time, to deliver and protect the site. This is separate from local gameplay storage. See Vercel's Privacy Notice.

Off Script does not add advertising, analytics or tracking scripts, contact forms, or remotely hosted third-party fonts to these pages. Browser storage and cached files support app functionality, not advertising. This statement does not mean that a hosting provider receives no network request information.

Opening GitHub support or another external link sends your browser to that service, where its own privacy practices apply. GitHub issue reports are public, and posting requires a GitHub account. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Content choices

Off Script offers content controls, including Classroom clean and optional adult comedy. These are local content preferences, not verification of a person's age or identity. The app does not require a date of birth or identity document to play.

Questions and privacy requests

Do not put player names, email addresses, private-role screenshots or sensitive request details in public issues. Requests about data held by Apple, Vercel or GitHub may also need to be addressed through that provider's privacy tools.

Support messages you choose to send are used to respond and investigate the issue. Keep them limited to relevant information. Copies are kept only while needed for support, app improvement or a legal obligation. If the app's data handling changes, this policy and its update date will be revised.
