YOUR PHONE IS ALREADY INVITED.

Android? You're in.

Play the full browser version on a modern Android phone, iPhone, tablet or computer. No Android download or TestFlight invitation needed for the web version.

This is shared-device, in-person play—not an online multiplayer room. Names, saved prompts and games stay in the browser you use. Browser saves do not sync with the iPhone app or another device.

Open the game →

Keep it on your home screen.

  1. Open Play online in your browser.
  2. On Android Chrome, use the browser menu's Install app or Add to Home screen option, if offered.
  3. On iPhone Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen.

The first visit needs internet. Open the game and let its files finish caching before relying on offline play. Offline access depends on your browser keeping those files; private browsing or clearing site data can remove them.
