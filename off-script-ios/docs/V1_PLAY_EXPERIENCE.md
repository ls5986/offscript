# V1 play experience

Off Script keeps three independent entry points:

- **Prompt Studio:** generate a place or any selected ingredient combination, without creating a game session.
- **Party Night:** play with the remembered group and material settings.
- **Explore Games:** deliberately choose from the full eligible catalog.

Studio is visually first on Home; Party remains directly available. Names are optional. The app remembers safe Party comfort options and game selections, but a new group/session must affirm adult consent again.

## Low-tap quick games

The default quick mix contains Pickup Lines, World's Worst, Terrible Advice and Never Say That. The adult analogy game is available only with affirmative group consent. Other games remain available through deliberate selection and the included-games chooser.

Everyone uses the same input. How to Play suggests the reader starts and
everyone tries a separate response or passes, but the group can keep going
for more attempts. The phone does **not** listen to answers or require a Done
tap between speakers.

- **Another word / job / place / prompt** stays in the current quick game.
- **Change game** keeps the group and explicitly selects an activity.
- **Shuffle included games** is optional. It is not the default.
- **Choose games** changes future eligible games, preserving the current prompt until the next deliberate action.
- **Party menu → Hide this game type** removes the current activity, including
  all its material variants, from this party's selection and the remembered
  selection for future parties on this device. The current card/history stays
  intact; the next action uses another selected game. Choose games can include
  it again. Hiding the last selected game leaves an explicit empty deck with
  Edit deck recovery, never an automatic fallback to unwanted games.
- A game selected outside the default quick mix is included for the current session; it does not rewrite the remembered default for future groups.
- **Use default mix** restores the quick selection. The app does not replay excluded games afterward.

The optional turn guide supports people who prefer named story turns. It is not a required tap after each spoken line.

## Swipe through prompts

Studio and quick Party cards support deliberate horizontal browsing:

- **Swipe left:** open the next saved prompt, or generate another at the end.
- **Swipe right:** restore the exact previous prompt.
- **Previous prompt** and the existing next/generate button provide alternatives.
- A normal tap does not advance anything. Vertical touch movement still scrolls.
- History holds up to thirty exact snapshots and survives reopening/reload.

Back changes only the displayed prompt, not the game, cast, turn progress,
scores or timer. Forward revisits saved words rather than creating a replacement.
An individual reroll from an older prompt starts a new branch.

Changing Studio categories, performer count, locks, output/selection preferences
or recipes starts a fresh browsing context, so Back cannot undo that choice.
Changing the Party cast, material settings, included games or round invalidates
old browsing context. Current content restrictions still apply.

Gestures ignore ordinary button presses, predominantly vertical movement,
multi-finger gestures, short drags and starts within 24 pixels of a screen edge.
They are disabled during menus, private handoffs, scenes and New Choice caller
controls. Swiping is prompt browsing, not a hidden Change game or New Choice cue.

## Activities have different jobs

Independent one-liners, scenes, stories, interviews, private-role games and movement exercises use their own participation instructions in How to Play. Character roles are attached to names where that matters. The phone should not pretend that every activity has the same next-player operation.

Scene timers and suggested starting lengths are guidance, not cutoffs or speech
recognition. Groups can keep playing beyond a suggested number of lines, turns,
questions or seconds; they choose when to generate another scene/story. Timer
expiry leaves the current Party card live. Half-Life in individual practice
retains its specific timed replay structure.

For New Choice's named human caller, minimum group, scene cast and bell behavior, see [New Choice and host roles](NEW_CHOICE_AND_HOST_ROLES.md).

## Task-fit prompts

The Party play screen keeps **End game** in the fixed bottom controls, beside
Pause and How to Play. It ends the current party in one tap and opens its saved
recap without deleting prompts or history. It remains available while paused,
in Large Display and when a card is blocked by content/player requirements.
The Party menu still offers End Session and the separate Save & leave action.

The recap explicitly confirms local saving and links to **View saved parties**.
Saved opens **Sessions** when there are saved sessions instead of landing on
an empty favorites view. Its Games, Prompts and other tabs remain available.
No save or extra confirmation step was added to End game.

**Change game** and the individual free-play picker include short existing
game descriptions, not names alone. A first-ever Party scene offers
**Learn this game** before Start, opening the same How to Play sheet.
It is not shown during live play or repeated scenes; the footer help remains.
This is based on the saved round history, not an account or analytics flag.

Content pack **2.7.0** separates independent ingredients from complete
game-specific advice problems and rewrites bundled scene seeds into neutral
cores. Matching material is selected after cast eligibility; a neutral core is
not mislabeled adult merely to retain an old quantity target.
See [material levels](PARTY_MATERIAL_AND_HINTS.md).

Opening-line hints have been removed from cards, individual games and help.
Party cards show the creative input, cast/roles, any playable joke frame or
advice question, and essential live controls. The House rule badge,
turn-by-turn directions, stopping paragraphs and advice examples are not on the
card. How to Play contains the instructions, examples and optional starting
length. Game-format starters remain; optional opening hints do not return.

Party and individual games use the shared game-input policy:

- World's Worst starts from recognizable jobs; whimsical jobs remain available in Studio.
- Terrible Advice receives an actual first-person problem, with an explicit advice request and matching example.
- Association starts with simple objects rather than elaborate props.
- Word-at-a-Time does not require an unnecessary scene setup.
- Who, What, Where and Make the Mundane Matter retain the clues that define their tasks.
- Beginner emotion cues are available without deleting more nuanced acting ingredients.

An Open Scene without a selected relationship explicitly invites players to
invent their own characters. Action-only inputs say to start with the selected
action; no people, relationship or extra ingredient is silently supplied.
This instruction is consistent across the intro, live scene and Rules &
coaching. Timed Open Scene rounds show an optional reminder duration before
Start, with permission to keep playing. Timing behavior and free-play choices
are unchanged.

Ordinary words remain useful for Pickup Lines and the adult analogy frame. A higher material setting does not require explicit nouns, real-player disclosures or dares. Changing the Party material level selects an appropriate variant when the current game has one.

## Studio output and compatibility

**Act out this prompt** carries the exact Studio snapshot into Open Scene;
it does not fill in omitted categories. Open Scene's New Prompt and replay
rounds keep that ingredient selection, flavor, selection style and composition
preference. For example, Location + Relationship stays those two categories,
without adding a Situation. Choosing a different game uses that game's inputs.
Studio's ensemble intent persists across replay, reload and new cast/setup,
using all active people rather than silently reverting to a pair. Ordinary
Open Scene still casts its normal pair.

Independent Actions tagged as direct-transitive bind an explicitly selected
Object in optional composition, for example Repairing + A whistle becomes
Repairing a whistle. No Object is invented when that category was not selected.
The requested Playful flavor comes before soft associations, so Connected
ideas cannot erase its one available playful twist. Locks remain exact.

Content **2.7** neutralizes workplace/job/subject details in relationships and
their named/expandable roles together. Equivalent records consolidate within
compatible cast ranges. Earlier captured wording is preserved until an
explicit unlocked Update ingredients action. All 1,326 source records remain
available for provenance, and the unchanged full advice bank remains available
to its game. See [the authoring and preservation guide](ATOMIC_27_CONTENT.md).

Fresh Studio is ingredients-first. **Combine into a scene** is an explicit preference, separate from Connected ideas / Mix freely selection. Turning composition off does not change which categories were requested.

Locks and individual rerolls preserve the other exact entries. Large Display retains generation. Saved prompts preserve their captured words, versions, roles and output preference; older saves without the new optional flag retain legacy composition behavior.

Content pack **2.5.0** includes the bounded input/grammar/task review, with immutable 2.3/2.4 editorial records and preserved original text. New numbered ensemble assignments are unique. Explicit editable-copy upgrades may repair unlocked old labels; ordinary load and locked saves are not rewritten.

## Feedback

**Report a problem** opens a clearly named feedback sheet, separate from
**How to Play**. It prepares a minimal report with the game, app version,
source prompt IDs, a selected issue and an optional note. It does not
automatically include player names, actor UUIDs, saved prompt prose or private
roles.

On web, **Copy feedback** copies the report; nothing is automatically submitted. On native, **Share feedback** opens the platform share flow; delivery depends on the app/recipient the user selects. Existing quick ratings remain local unless deliberately exported/shared.

For an iOS beta, TestFlight is the direct developer-feedback channel for screenshot/comments. A production support destination must be supplied and approved by the owner; no address or backend has been invented.

## Release and verification limits

No accounts, analytics, recording or remote generation were added. Studio remains useful for solo writers/actors and teachers without a class roster. Guided rosters remain capped at twelve; a classroom with twenty students can still use prompts for a smaller active cast.
