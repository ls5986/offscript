### Description

```text
Make it up as you go.

Off Script brings improv games and scene ideas to the people in your room. Share one phone, try a small offer, and see where it goes. No script or Off Script account required.

PARTY NIGHT
Set your group and deal a prompt. Everyone tries the same idea, then chooses another. Stay with a favorite game or change things up when you want to. How to Play is always close by.

PROMPT STUDIO
Start with a place or choose your own mix of ingredients. Lock what you love, reroll the rest, and optionally combine your selections into a scene. Use the prompt for acting, writing, or a quick creative warm-up.

A LIBRARY OF STARTING POINTS
Browse more than 1,200 individual ingredients and ready-made prompt recipes. Search, filter, and use an idea directly in Studio.

NEW CHOICE, NEW POSSIBILITIES
Try games with wordplay, scenes, characters, and physical expression. New Choice brings actors and a separate caller together: when the caller asks for a new choice, try another version of the last line.

KEEP THE GOOD STUFF
Save exact prompts, favorite games, and come back to party recaps. Saves stay on this device; there is no app-managed cloud sync.

YOUR RULES. YOUR ROOM.
Choose Clean, Cheeky, or optional 18+ content for your group. Classroom clean keeps higher-rated content hidden. Any game can be skipped; drinking, touching, and personal disclosures are never required.

BUILT FOR THE ROOM YOU ARE IN
Ordinary gameplay works offline after installation. Use larger text, Large Display, reduced motion, and optional spoken cues and haptics to make play comfortable. Appearance follows your device's light or dark setting.

Party Night supports groups of 2-12, with eligible games depending on group size. Some formats need more people: New Choice requires at least three, including a separate caller.

Less screen. More scene.
```

## App Review notes

```text
Off Script is an in-person improv and creative-prompt app. People share one device. It is not online multiplayer and does not require an Off Script account, server, subscription, purchase, or demo login.

Suggested review:
1. From Home, open Party Night, choose a player count, and tap Deal first card.
2. Use How to Play for instructions. Change game explains available formats. New Choice requires at least three people: actors and a separate caller. Its New Choice calls are manual, not automatic.
3. End game opens the saved recap. Saved > Sessions retains the recap on this device.
4. Open Prompt Studio for a standalone idea. Customize selects ingredients. Lock an ingredient, reroll another, or use Act out this prompt.
5. Open the Prompt & recipe library, search or filter, and tap Use to open an exact ingredient or recipe in Studio. Save prompt retains an exact copy under Saved > Prompts.
6. Ordinary gameplay works offline. Internet access is needed for installation, updates, and opening external privacy/support links.

Content: Clean is the initial gameplay level. The app also includes Cheeky and opt-in 18+ suggestive adult comedy. Adult consent/settings can be reached in the relevant content controls. Settings > Classroom clean enforces clean content, including saved prompts. These controls are not identity-based age verification. Higher-rated content must be considered in the age-rating questionnaire.

The app does not record audio or video or request microphone/camera access. Optional spoken cues use device speech, and sound/haptics have visible/manual alternatives.

Player names, sessions, favorites, prompts and notes are stored locally in SQLite. There is no developer-operated gameplay backend or app-managed cloud sync. Report a problem prepares feedback; the user chooses whether and where to share it.
```
